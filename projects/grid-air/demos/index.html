Grid Artificial Intelligence Resilience

GRID-AIR

Building security into the AI that will run the grid.

Smart grids are adopting AI for forecasting, control, and self-healing. GRID-AIR asks a prior question: can that AI be trusted? Nine interactive demonstrations make the defense-in-depth approach tangible, from the silicon up to the operator's console and the community it serves. Everything runs in your browser with simulated data.

Interactive demonstrations

click a card to launch · nine demos in a guided order

The order climbs the defense-in-depth stack: trusted hardware, then trusted data and model, then self-healing, then validation and operations, and finally what it all means for a community in a hurricane.

Scope. These demonstrations are for workforce development and awareness. All nine use simulated data and are visualizations of the approach, not the research systems. Attack timings, error rates, and trace counts are illustrative and tuned for live viewing.
