Press Start event. A disturbance will develop upstream. Some are real faults that will trip the feed; some are transients that pass. Island too early and you run on batteries for nothing; island too late and the microgrid goes dark on an unplanned separation.

Teaming lesson: the advisor is well calibrated but not perfect. It reaches 70% on most real faults with 20 to 40 s to spare, and on about one transient in twelve. Use its confidence, then check what it cannot see: whether the deviation is recovering, and whether your island can carry the load right now.
